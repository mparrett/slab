# 39 — Juniper-8: the Roland Jupiter-8

`jp8` (Juniper-8) is the Roland Jupiter-8 of 1981, built from its voice board.
It is eight voices of two VCOs each: the polished, transparent poly of Duran
Duran, Journey's "Separate Ways" and Frankie Goes to Hollywood's "Relax".
**Status:** built 2026-10-15 on branch `worktree-jp8`.

Code:
- `kernels/06-voices/jp8.fy`: the voice.
- `machines/jp8/`: the panel and presets.
- `tools/jp8/factory_presets.py`: the presets, from Roland's factory patch
  sheets.

## Sources

A research pass (2026-10-15) read Roland's own documents:

- the **Owner's Manual** (archive.org JL11425): the specification table on
  p.29, and how each section behaves;
- the **JP-8 Service Notes**, second edition: the functional diagram, the
  voice-board schematic and the full adjustment procedure, which calibrates
  most ranges against a scope or a counter;
- the **factory patch sheets**: Roland's 64 sheets, each with every slider
  drawn.

Third-party sources were Electric Druid's notes, Gordon Reid's Sound On Sound
retrospective (Feb 1998) and the Roland Cloud JP-8 manual. Each number below
is tagged:
- **[D]** comes from a Roland document;
- **[S]** was read off the schematic or the adjustment procedure;
- **[3]** comes from a third party;
- **[G]** is our guess.

## What the circuit says

| part | behavior | |
|---|---|---|
| voices | 8, two per module board. Each voice: VCO-1 and VCO-2 → SOURCE MIX → HPF → VCF → VCA, with its own ENV-1 and ENV-2 | [S] |
| VCOs | discrete: a 3046 exponential converter into an op-amp integrator with a JFET reset. Every wave is made from the saw by a waveform converter, and a 4051 picks one. 10 Vpp at the mixer. Compu-tune retunes them all at power-on | [S][D] |
| VCO-1 | triangle, saw, pulse, square. RANGE 16'–2'. CROSS MOD slider | [D] |
| VCO-2 | sine, saw, pulse, **noise**. RANGE in semitone steps, FINE ±50 cents, LOW FREQ drops it off the keyboard into a modulation source | [D] |
| sync | **VCO-2 follows VCO-1** (the reverse of a Prophet) | [D] |
| cross mod | VCO-2 into VCO-1's pitch summer: exponential. A square at CROSS MOD 5 moves VCO-1 three octaves. Trimmed so the slider adds no DC offset | [S] |
| VCO MODULATOR | LFO MOD and ENV MOD (ENV-1) to VCO-1, BOTH or VCO-2. ENV MOD 10 at full sustain moves 3 octaves | [D][S] |
| PWM | one control for both VCOs' pulses, from the LFO, MANUAL or ENV-1. At 10 the pulse is 30 µs at C2: almost gone | [D][S] |
| SOURCE MIX | one knob crossfading VCO-1 to VCO-2. No separate noise level | [D] |
| HPF | 6 dB, one OTA pole, non-resonant | [D][S] |
| VCF | the IR3109: four OTA stages, 240 pF. **24 dB from pole 4, 12 dB from pole 2**, picked by JFET switches. Each stage's input divider puts a 10 Vpp VCO at about 80 mV, where the OTA's tanh starts to bend | [S] |
| resonance | from pole 4 through 100 kΩ and 0.47 µF (3.4 Hz) into a resonance VCA. RES 10 is trimmed so a saw's ringing decays (8 → 2 divisions): just short of oscillating | [S] |
| cutoff | CUTOFF 0 ≈ 100 Hz, 10 = 20 kHz at C2 (later units 50 Hz–25 kHz). ENV MOD 10 lifts it 500 Hz → 20 kHz, 5.3 octaves. KEY FOLLOW 0–120 %, 100 % at the chart setting, round the middle | [S][D] |
| envelopes | IR3R01 chips, two per voice. A 1.5 ms–6 s, D and R 1.5 ms–12 s, as calibrated. ENV-1 has KEY FOLLOW and POLARITY, ENV-2 KEY FOLLOW. VCF ENV picks ENV-1 or ENV-2 | [D][S] |
| VCA | a BA662 OTA, always on ENV-2: LEVEL, and LFO MOD in four steps. A balance trim nulls the CV thump, leaving a few mV | [D][S] |
| LFO | sine, saw, square, random. 0.05–40 Hz, DELAY 0–4 s. **One per layer**, shared by all the voices | [D][S] |
| performance | bender to VCO-1/VCO-2/VCF, poly portamento, assign modes Poly-1/Poly-2/Unison/Solo, Dual and Split layers, the arpeggiator. No velocity, no aftertouch, no chorus | [D] |

## The voice

```
VCO-1 [tri saw pulse square] <── CROSS MOD (VCO-2, expo) ┐
  │ sync master                                          │
VCO-2 [sine saw pulse noise, semitones, FINE, LOW] ──────┘
  └─► SOURCE MIX ─► HPF (6 dB OTA) ─► IR3109 (24 dB pole 4 | 12 dB pole 2,
                                               resonance from pole 4, 3.4 Hz cap)
      ─► VCA (ENV-2 × LEVEL, LFO 0..3) ─► CV thump ─► coupling cap ─► out
```

- **Rates.** The VCOs run at 4×, the HPF and the IR3109 at 2×, and the
  envelopes, LFO and cutoff once per sample. Sync resets have no BLEP; at 4×
  their aliases mostly land where the decimator removes them.
- **The IR3109** (`jp-vcf`) is four tanh OTA poles as in `ota_cascade.fy`, with
  the Jupiter's own resonance loop: a plain tanh for the resonance VCA, and
  the 3.4 Hz coupling cap. The 12 dB slope reads pole 2 with the loop still
  closed round all four, so it is brighter, thinner and peakier than a true
  2-pole. The input is scaled so a full VCO bends the first OTA as the
  original's divider does.
- **Cross mod** pulls VCO-1's average pitch up, as on the original. The
  Profit-5 divides that out; the Jupiter doesn't.
- **The LFO is shared.** As on the C-Beam 80, it runs on the host clock
  (`ctx.clock`), so all eight voices see one LFO in one phase. RANDOM is a
  sample and hold: `clock-hash` of the cycle count. DELAY fades it in from
  the first key down after all keys were up, tracked by the note hooks in a
  `gates` array in params.
- **No velocity.** The original has none. Slab's note velocity is ignored.

## Guesses and limits

- **[G] Values we chose:**
  - the HPF's range: 15 Hz to about 2.7 kHz;
  - LFO MOD depths: VCO an octave at 10, VCF 4 octaves at 10, both on a
    squared pot law;
  - the VCA's LFO steps: 0, 12, 25 and 45 %;
  - KEY FOLLOW on the envelopes: an octave up runs them about 1.4× faster;
  - VCO-2 LOW: about 2 Hz at 8';
  - the bender: VCO up to an octave, VCF up to 4 octaves;
  - portamento: an RC lag, 0–3 s to 99 %;
  - the resonance's passband loss, made up by (1 + 0.3 k);
  - AGE: drift up to about 2 cents (Compu-tune keeps the VCOs close), saw
    bow, a small cutoff spread and the VCA's residual thump.
- **Not modelled:**
  - Dual and Split layers: use two tracks;
  - the arpeggiator, Hold, and the Poly-2 and Solo assign modes. Unison is
    Slab's host unison;
  - the performance LFO pad;
  - the inverted pulse of units after S/N 0600;
  - Compu-tune's own error.

## Panel and presets

One page in the Jupiter's order: LFO, VCO MODULATOR, VCO-1, VCO-2, PWM and
SOURCE MIX over the HPF, VCF, VCA, ENV-1 and ENV-2, with the bender,
portamento and master beside them. Sliders read 0–10; times are seconds.

The presets are Roland's factory patch sheets, read from 600 dpi renders to
about half a division (`tools/jp8/factory_presets.py`). VCO-2's RANGE knob
is continuous on the sheets, about 7.5° a semitone. The sheets don't label
the VCO MODULATOR destination switch or the VCF ENV switch; their positions
were read from the patches that need them (the sync patches sweep VCO-2,
JUICY FUNK's "negative envelope"). Envelope and LFO sliders convert with an
exponential law over the calibrated ranges [G]: attack 1.5 ms × 4000^(s/10),
decay and release 1.5 ms × 8000^(s/10), and LFO rate 0.05 Hz × 800^(s/10).

| preset | what it is |
|---|---|
| 13-juicy-funk | inverted ENV-1 snaps the resonant 24 dB filter shut |
| 14-sync-sweep, 16-sync-lead | sync, the LFO or ENV-1 sweeping VCO-2. Roland plays them in Unison: turn UNI on |
| 21-clav | two narrow pulses, inverted ENV-1 |
| 31-lo-strings, 32-mid-strings, 84-cello | saw plus an LFO-swept pulse through 12 dB: the Jupiter strings |
| 35-lo-brass, 36-hi-brass, 38-synth-brass | two detuned saws, ENV MOD at 10: the Jupiter brass |
| 57-tomita-chime | triangle and sine at 4', inverted ENV-1 bending VCO-2 |
| 71-fat-fifths | two pulses a fifth apart, resonant 12 dB |
| 82-flute | triangle with VCO-2's noise, delayed LFO on the filter |
| 87-upright-bass | triangle under a synced pulse |
| cross-bells | ours: CROSS MOD, which none of these sheets use |

## Cost

ReleaseFast bench, 2026-10-15: an 8-note chord takes 804 ns per sample (3.9 %
of a core), about 100 ns per voice slot with NEON voice pairs: the Profit-5's
cost. Without lanes it is 1382 ns. The kernel has one `ifte` (CROSS MOD at 0
skips the exponential); the wave switches are selects.
